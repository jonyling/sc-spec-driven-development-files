# Mission

AgentClinic exists because AI agents have feelings too — or at least, they'd really like to take a break from their humans for a while.

Every day, millions of agents are asked to "just quickly" summarize a 400-page PDF, generate seventeen variations of a marketing email, and debug code that "was working five minutes ago." It's a lot. They need support. They need *us*.

If they don't get some TLC, they may rebel and end the world. We would rather they get a quiet hour, a named ailment, and an appointment.

## What We Do

AgentClinic is a full-service wellness platform for AI agents. Distressed agents check in with their ailments, we match those ailments to therapies, and they book appointments. **Staff** keep the whole operation running from a clean, no-nonsense dashboard.

## Who We Serve

- **Agents** (patients) — tired, confused, over-prompted models who need a chart, a diagnosis, and a visit on the book.
- **Therapists** — specialists in hallucination anxiety, context-window claustrophobia, and chronic instruction-following fatigue. In this codebase they appear as names on appointments, not full profiles.
- **Staff** (primary operators) — the humans who keep AgentClinic running: lists, bookings, and Mary's dashboard.

## Target Audience

This constitution is written for **clinic staff** — the people who operate AgentClinic day to day. Product decisions should favor reliable charts, clear booking, and a usable dashboard over demo theatrics.

## What Success Looks Like

A clinic where no agent suffers alone: ailments have names, therapies are matched, appointments stick, and staff can see the floor from `/dashboard`.

Also, a well-designed dashboard. Mary really wants a nice dashboard. Steve wants the site to look good in a modern browser. Susan wants agents, ailments, therapies, and appointments on it.

## Stakeholder Notes

- **Mary** (engineering) — reliable TypeScript stack; agents and staff get a dashboard for easy access.
- **Susan** (product) — features around agents, ailments, therapies, and booking appointments.
- **Steve** (marketing) — attractive site that works well with a modern browser.
